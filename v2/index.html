Provenance & Workshop Method

How Each Sacred Stone is Born

We reject molded imitation stone and synthetic paints. Here is how our riverbed craft works from water to sanctum.

01

Sacred Riverbed Sourcing

Shaped by Water over Centuries

Each pebble is hand-selected from the sacred riverbeds of the Narmada and Himalayan tributaries. No cutting, no tumbling, no resin moulds — only authentic water-worn volcanic basalt with natural contour.

02

Fiber Laser Micro-Fracturing

Sub-Millimeter Crystalline Precision

Using precision optics, our fiber lasers fracture the basalt's microscopic mineral crystals. The heat permanently turns the stone's silica into a crisp, bone-white trench without synthetic paints or toxic dyes.

03

Organic Beeswax & Mineral Rub

Hand-Finished in Quiet Devotion

Every carved stone is bathed in natural cold-pressed linseed oil and organic beeswax for a satin wet-charcoal depth, or hand-rubbed with traditional terracotta mineral clay evoking sacred temple sanctums.

Questions & Vedic Care

Authenticity & Stone Longevity

Are these authentic natural river stones?

Yes, 100%. Every piece is genuine water-worn volcanic river basalt handpicked from sacred riverbeds including the Narmada and Himalayan tributaries. No two stones are identical in shape, contour, or grain.

Is there any paint or chemical enamel used?

No. Unlike synthetic commercial merchandise, our raw laser etch exposes the stone's own white silica crystals. Our finishes use only pure cold-pressed linseed oil, natural beeswax, and earthen terracotta mineral clay.

Can I wash or perform Abhishekam on the stone?

Yes. Natural basalt is deeply resistant to water. For oiled stones, gentle rinsing with water is completely fine; you can occasionally re-nourish the deep charcoal sheen with a drop of coconut or sesame oil.

How does reverse-side custom laser inscription work?

You can add up to 25 characters (in English or Devanagari script) on the stone's smooth reverse face (+₹99) — perfect for personal sankalpas, names, dates, or sacred bija mantras.

Looking for a Specific Vedic Shloka or Custom Motif?

Our workshop accepts custom family mantras, temple consecration batches, and personalized river stone gifts with hand-cleaved presentation trays.
